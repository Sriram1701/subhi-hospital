About Us

Subhi Subhiksham Hospitals

Subhi Subhiksham Hospitals Pvt Ltd is a premier multi-speciality healthcare institute dedicated to delivering ethical, patient-centric, and high-quality medical services to the people of Cuddalore and surrounding regions. Equipped with modern intensive care, 24/7 laboratory, pharmacy, and expert doctors across 18 specialities, we strive to bring healing, hope, and health to every family.

Comprehensive Healthcare Under One Roof

We provide consultation, emergency care, inpatient treatment, surgical services, critical care and diagnostic support across multiple specialties.

Our Approach to Patient Care

At Subhi Subhiksham Hospitals, we believe good healthcare is not only about treating illness—it is about understanding the patient and family, communicating clearly and providing the right care at the right time.

We strive to maintain a healthcare environment that is safe, respectful, transparent and affordable.
